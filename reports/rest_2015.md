# A separate 2015 REST model: ET is not recognisable at rest, and PADS transfer reverses the ranking

**Question.** Rest tremor is the PD-specific clinical sign (and, clinically, the
one that tends to respond to dopaminergic treatment while ET does not). Does a
separate 2015 REST model -- never combined with OUT -- classify N / PD / ET, and
does transfer from PADS's rest task (Relaxed) help it as PADS StretchHold helped
the OUT model?

**Answer.** No on both. ET precision at rest is at chance (0.124 at prevalence
0.106) in every arm. PADS transfer drives ET-vs-PD ranking **below** chance
(AUC 0.484, −0.105 \* vs scratch, worse in 39 of 40 repeats) and is worse than
a permuted-label control, so PADS's rest-tremor labels teach the wrong rule for
2015 patients. Transfer still helps tremor detection (precN +0.041 \* over the
control).

Run: `python -m experiments.rest_2015 diag` (pre-check), then
`TREMOR_DEVICE=cuda STAGE=deep LAMBDA=1 REPS=a-b python -m experiments.rest_2015`
in parts and `STAGE=deep python -m experiments.rest_2015 report`.

## Pre-check (linear, no deep fits)

2015 REST: 151 patients, 60 N / 75 PD / 16 ET (the four `N 2` accelerometer
files are skipped by the loader). Features as the OUT pipeline, lower-arm sensor.

| axis | PADS Relaxed → 2015 (fitted once) | 2015 own CV |
|---|---|---|
| N vs tremor | 0.637 (null top 0.592) | 0.788 (null top 0.620) |
| PD vs ET | **0.495** (null [0.346, 0.656]) | 0.605-0.620 (null top 0.675-0.692) |

PD vs ET does not transfer at all, as the opposite frequency rule predicts
(PADS ET slower than PD at rest, 2015 ET faster). **2015's own PD-vs-ET REST
signal is weaker than `inhouse_pd_vs_et.md` reported**: across feature sets
(all 159 columns, spectrum + descriptors, spectrum only, the six
characteristics) it reads AUC 0.605-0.620 with p 0.10-0.12; that report's
settings gave 0.650, p 0.025. Borderline and analysis-dependent, not
established.

## Deep model, 40 CV partitions

Same protocol as `transfer_2015` (OOF on all 151, per-fold validation for early
stopping and offsets, reported recipe x 3 seeds, GPU).

| arm | precN | precPD | precET | macroP | macroF1 | PD-vs-ET AUC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **scratch** (2015 REST only) | **0.673** | **0.707** | **0.124** | **0.501** | **0.492** | **0.588** |
| ft (pretrain PADS Relaxed) | 0.659 | 0.679 | 0.114 | 0.484 | 0.475 | 0.484 |
| ft_shuf (pretrain, labels permuted) | 0.619 | 0.683 | 0.117 | 0.473 | 0.459 | 0.551 |
| l2sp_1 (ft + L2-SP λ 1) | 0.642 | 0.655 | 0.114 | 0.470 | 0.459 | 0.462 |

Paired:

| contrast | precN | precPD | precET | macroP | PD-vs-ET AUC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ft − scratch | −0.013 | −0.028 \* | −0.010 | −0.017 \* | **−0.105 \*** (win 0.03) |
| ft − ft_shuf | **+0.041 \*** | −0.004 | −0.003 | +0.011 | **−0.067 \*** |
| l2sp_1 − scratch | −0.030 \* | −0.053 \* | −0.010 | −0.031 \* | **−0.126 \*** |

## Reading it

* **The REST recordings do not carry a usable PD-vs-ET signal for this model.**
  Even from scratch, precET is 1.2x chance and the ranking AUC 0.588.
* **PADS actively misleads on PD vs ET at rest.** ft is below chance and below
  the permuted-label control; anchoring to the PADS weights (L2-SP) makes it
  worse. This is the opposite-frequency-rule finding reproduced inside the deep
  model, and the mirror image of OUT, where the same transfer helped.
* **What does transfer:** tremor presence. ft beats its control on precN.
* **Why rest fails here** is the same account as `rest_postural_contrast.md`
  (git history): 74 % of PD patients show *more* postural than rest tremor, so
  the recordings are not eliciting rest tremor -- plausibly because patients
  were ON dopaminergic medication, which suppresses it, and the protocol has no
  distraction task. None of the three cohorts records medication state.

## Standing

* Keep one model per action; the 2015 OUT transfer model is the useful one.
  Do not use PADS Relaxed as a transfer source for REST.
* The rest-tremor axis needs data, not modelling: medication state and time
  since last dose per recording, ideally paired OFF / ON rest recordings with
  the limb supported and a distraction task. The OFF-to-ON change in rest-tremor
  amplitude would be a direct feature (ET should not change); note PD tremor's
  dopaminergic response is itself variable, so it is specific rather than
  sensitive.
