# PD vs ET on 2015 OUT: coupling in a second stage lifts the ranking to AUC 0.71 -- confirmed on fresh partitions

Run: `python -m experiments.pdet_2015` (selection partitions, seeds 0-39);
confirmation: `FUSION_DIR=fusion_2015_confirm SEG_DIR=none python -m
experiments.pdet_2015` after `REPS=100-140 OUT_DIR=fusion_2015_confirm python
-m experiments.fusion_2015`.

## Selection partitions (40 repeats, the partitions every 2015 arm was tuned on)

PD vs ET among the 90 tremor patients (15 ET). Second-stage arms: logistic
regression on the OUT + REST fusion model's PD-vs-ET logit (+ features),
independent 5-fold CV per repeat. **Within-fold scoring** (per-fold AUC averaged;
most / two most ET-like patients per fold), because the five second-stage fold
models have different calibration and pooled scores mix scales -- pooled, even a
one-feature monotone refit "lost" 0.023 AUC; within folds it equals the base
exactly (0.660 = 0.660).

| arm | fold AUC | top-1 / fold | top-2 / fold |
|---|---|---|---|
| ft | 0.625 | 0.465 | 0.358 |
| fuse (OUT + REST) | 0.660 | 0.365 | 0.335 |
| ft_seg (coupling in the network) | 0.638 | **0.485** | **0.390** |
| **fuse -> + coupling8** | **0.712** | 0.275 | 0.360 |
| fuse -> + coupling8, rows shuffled | 0.564 | 0.205 | 0.225 |
| fuse -> + coupling8 + tremor amplitude (post hoc) | 0.699 | 0.205 | 0.343 |

| contrast | fold AUC | top-1 | top-2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| + coupling8 − ft | **+0.087 \*** | −0.190 \* | +0.002 |
| + coupling8 − fuse | **+0.051 \*** | −0.090 \* | +0.025 |
| + coupling8 − shuffled | **+0.148 \*** | +0.070 | **+0.135 \*** |
| ft_seg − ft | **+0.013 \*** | +0.020 | **+0.033 \*** |
| + amplitude − + coupling8 | −0.012 \* | −0.070 \* | −0.017 |

## Reading (selection partitions)

* **Overall PD-vs-ET discrimination improves most with coupling in a second
  stage**: AUC 0.625 -> 0.712, the largest PD-vs-ET gain measured on 2015, and
  far above its shuffled control.
* **Its single most confident ET call per fold is worse** than `ft` / `ft_seg`
  (0.275 vs 0.465 / 0.485); for flagging a few patients, `ft_seg` is best.
* The post-hoc amplitude interaction ("coherence means nothing without tremor")
  made it worse -- registered as failed.

## Confirmation on fresh partitions (seeds 100-139, nothing changed)

| arm | fold AUC (selection) | **fold AUC (fresh)** | top-1 / fold (fresh) | top-2 / fold (fresh) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ft | 0.625 | 0.636 | **0.435** | **0.393** |
| fuse | 0.660 | 0.678 | 0.390 | 0.373 |

| fuse -> + coupling8, shuffled | 0.564 | 0.575 | 0.215 | 0.230 |
| + amplitude (post hoc) | 0.699 | 0.697 | 0.235 | 0.335 |

| contrast (fresh) | fold AUC | top-1 | top-2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| + coupling8 − ft | **+0.074 \*** | −0.150 \* | −0.020 |
| + coupling8 − fuse | **+0.033 \*** | −0.105 \* | +0.000 |
| + coupling8 − shuffled | **+0.136 \*** | +0.070 | **+0.142 \*** |

**The AUC gain replicates** (0.712 -> 0.710; +0.074 \* over `ft`, +0.033 \* over
fuse), unlike L2-SP's selection-partition gain in `transfer_2015.md`. The
top-1 weakness replicates too, and so does the amplitude variant's failure.

## Verdict

* **For PD-vs-ET discrimination (ROC / AUC, ranking the whole tremor
  population): fuse -> coupling second stage**, AUC 0.636 -> 0.710 on fresh
  partitions -- the largest confirmed PD-vs-ET gain on 2015.
* **For flagging the one or two most ET-like patients: `ft` / `ft_seg`**; the
  second stage's most confident pick is less often ET.
* Predictions: + coupling8 beats fuse and the shuffled control by +0.02-0.05
  -- held vs fuse (+0.033 / +0.051 within folds), exceeded vs the control
  (+0.136 / +0.148); avg gains < +0.015 -- held (−0.006). (AE). The post-hoc
  amplitude interaction failed twice (#41).

## Follow-up: can the AUC gain become ET precision? No (`rerank_2015.py`)

Fixed operating point: the recommended `fuse_w25` makes its 3-class calls; only
*which* of its tremor calls receive its ET labels (same count per fold) is
decided by the coupling second stage, fitted on the other four folds. A rerank
by `fuse_w25`'s own logit reproduces the base exactly (sanity check).

| partitions | rerank − base precET | precPD | rerank − shuffled-coupling precET |
|---|---|---|---|
| selection (0-39) | −0.033 | +0.011 \* | +0.084 \* |
| fresh (100-139) | −0.031 \* | +0.006 | +0.101 \* |

The coupling ranking is real (far above its shuffled control) but does not pick
better ET than the base at ~3 ET calls per fold: its AUC gain is in the middle of
the ranking, while the base's top picks (sharp ET tremor) stay the better ET
calls. Prediction (+0.03-0.08 over base) failed: #42. **Use the coupling second
stage for PD-vs-ET discrimination (AUC), not for the ET decision.**
